Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Milton Station LLC (“Milton Station,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is an Oregon limited liability company (registry 265247396). We operate a package-receiving and pickup service for private-carrier deliveries at 85462 Hwy 11, Milton-Freewater, OR 97862. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how long we keep it, and how you can contact us.

Information we collect

We collect information you provide and information created when we receive or release a package, including:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Package and tracking information
  • ID verification at pickup

We collect this information when you create an account, have a package shipped to us, contact us, or pick up a package.

How we use information

We use this information to:

  • Receive, log, photograph, hold, and release packages
  • Verify identity at pickup
  • Send text and email notices when a package arrives, before any storage fees apply, and about your account
  • Operate, secure, and improve the service
  • Meet legal, accounting, and recordkeeping obligations

Text messages and mobile information

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

If you opt in to the Milton Station Package Notifications program, we use your mobile number to send the messages described in our SMS Terms. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as needed to provide the service — for example, with vendors who send text or email messages on our behalf, subject to the mobile-information restriction above — to release a package to you or a person you authorize, or when we are required to do so by law.

Retention

We keep account, package, notice, and pickup records for as long as needed to provide the service, resolve disputes, and meet legal and recordkeeping obligations. When we no longer need the information, we delete or de-identify it.

Your choices

You may update your name, phone number, or email by contacting us. You may opt out of text messages by replying STOP, or request help by replying HELP. You may also email us to ask what information we have about you or to request that we delete it, subject to records we must retain.
