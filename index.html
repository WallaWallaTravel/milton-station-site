How it works

Ship it here. We hold it securely. You pick it up with ID.

We receive packages and hold them for pickup. We are not a U.S. Postal Service mail facility, and we do not provide mailboxes or USPS mail service.

01

Ship to us

Have your private carrier deliver the package to Milton Station at 85462 Hwy 11, Milton-Freewater, OR 97862. We accept UPS, FedEx, DHL, Amazon, and freight or pallet shipments.

02

We notify you

We’ll text and email you when your package arrives, and again before any storage fees apply.

03

Pick up with ID

Come in, show your ID, and we’ll hand over the package. We’ll only release it to you or someone you authorize.

What to expect

We keep them secure until you pick them up.

Logged and photographed

Each package is logged and photographed when it arrives, so we have a record of what came in.

ID-checked pickup

We check ID at pickup before a package goes out.

Notices before fees

We text and email you when a package arrives, and again before any storage fees apply.
