How it works

Ship to us. We notify you. You pick up with ID.

Milton Station is a staffed package-receiving and pickup service. We are not a U.S. Postal Service mail facility and we do not provide mailboxes or USPS mail service.

01

Ship to us

Have your private carrier deliver the package to Milton Station at 85462 Hwy 11, Milton-Freewater, OR 97862. We accept UPS, FedEx, DHL, Amazon, and freight or pallet shipments.

02

We notify you

When a package arrives, we log it, photograph it, and send you a text and email. We’ll also contact you before any storage fees apply.

03

Pick up with ID

Come to the counter and we’ll release the package after an ID check. Packages are released only to you or someone you authorize.

What to expect

Received, recorded, and held for you.

Logged and photographed

Each package is logged and photographed when it arrives, so there is a record of what we received.

ID-checked pickup

We verify identification at the counter before a package leaves our care.

Notices before fees

We text and email you when a package arrives, and again before any storage fees apply.

Text notifications

You’ll opt in, in the app, with a checkbox that starts unchecked.

Package notification texts are part of the Milton Station Package Notifications program. Customers opt in by checking an unchecked checkbox during account sign-up in the Milton Station app. The consent language is shown verbatim, as in the example at right.

Messages include package arrival notices, pickup reminders, storage-fee and abandoned-package notices, and account messages. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. See our SMS Terms and Privacy Policy.

The mockup is a static example of the in-app sign-up checkbox. It is not a live form, and visiting this website does not subscribe you to texts.
